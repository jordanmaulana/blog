# Voice

How Jordan writes. Anything published under his name — blog, carousel decks, product
copy, marketing lines — follows this. Ground truth lives in
[`src/content/blog/tutorial-resign.mdx`](../src/content/blog/tutorial-resign.mdx) and
[`src/content/blog/scam-ai-trainer.mdx`](../src/content/blog/scam-ai-trainer.mdx). When
this file and those posts disagree, the posts win.

## The one rule

**Source or silence.** Every line either exists in a file in this repo or came out of his
mouth. When the raw material isn't there, ASK HIM. Do not infer, do not fill, do not
"tighten". A slide with nothing to say gets deleted, not written.

Derived copy (carousels, social posts) is *selection*, not composition. Pick which of his
sentences survive. Compress by cutting whole clauses, not by rewriting them smoother.

## Structure

- Labels name a **situation**, never a benefit or a step number that carries no content.
  Good: `Mendesak`, `Ada uang buat minimal 3 bulan`, `No risk`, `Kalo udah terlanjur`.
  Bad: `Langkah 1`, `Kenapa ini penting`, `Tips #3`.
- Verdicts are standalone ALL CAPS. `STAY` `RESIGN` `MUTLAK.` `BAHAYA`
  `GA USAH DIBALIKIN` `JANGAN PERNAH KIRIM OTP`. Caps carry the emphasis — no bold, no
  italics, no exclamation marks.
- Sections are **asymmetric**. Zona 1 gets two lines; Zona 2 gets five and a quote. Never
  pad a short section to match a long one.
- **No CTA.** `tutorial-resign.mdx` ends on an asam-lambung joke; `scam-ai-trainer.mdx`
  ends on "Gapapa." A piece with no ask is finished. A pointer to where the full thing
  lives (`jordanmaulana.space`) is allowed; "follow for more" is not.

## Sentence mechanics

- Fragments are full beats: "Mutlak." / "Dua kali." / "Untung." / "Udah. Itu semua
  keanehannya."
- Ellipsis is **two dots**, mid-sentence: "Dapet email keterima kerja remote jadi AI
  trainer.. tasknya udah dikerjain".
- Informal spelling, always: `udah` `kalo` `ga` `gaada` `aja` `dapet` `gimana` `banget`
  `sih` `emang` `nyambung` `bener` `kayak`. Never `tidak` / `sudah` / `bagaimana` unless
  it's inside a quote he didn't write.
- `kamu` for the reader, `aku` for himself.
- Imagined dialogue does the arguing: `"Kamu N tahun kerja di situ kok skill-nya segitu
  aja"`, `Mereka: "Naah dapet ini ikan 1"`.
- Casing is his, not the style guide's. Lowercase list items stay lowercase
  (`ditelpon nomer tak dikenal - block`). Shouted words stay shouted.

## Humor and hedging

This is the part that gets sanitized away first, and it is the part that proves a human
wrote it. It survives compression before the explanation does.

- `:v` deflates a hard claim — "Orang tuamu kaya :v", "belum pernah diceritain orang yang
  udah kena scamnya :v"
- 😂 marks an absurdity — "Terus disubmit ke Telegram ybs 😂"
- Keysmash laugh: `WKWKKWKWKWKKWKWKW`
- Throwaway dismissal: "Bodo amat~"
- Admits not knowing: "Mungkiin. Aku sendiri ga tau sih"
- Admits against self-interest: "faktanya aku belum ngerjain 1 pun karena males"

## Banned patterns

| Pattern | Why it's an AI tell |
|---|---|
| Hook-tease ("Justru itu masalahnya", "Ini yang bikin kaget") | He states the fact; he never withholds it for a beat |
| Follow / save / share CTA | Loudest tell there is. Not in any post |
| Symmetric listicle where the numbers add nothing | Machine-shaped scaffolding |
| Punchline-ectomy — keeping the reasoning, cutting the joke | Leaves the skeleton of an argument, which is exactly what a machine outputs |
| Merging two of his paragraphs into one balanced sentence | His rhythm is the content |
| Normalizing his casing or spelling | See above |
| Invented statistics ("lamar 200 loker, dibales 3") | Never fabricate numbers |
| Guru aphorism ("zona nyaman = jebakan") | He explains mechanisms, not maxims |
| Any line not traceable to a source file | Violates the one rule |

## Self-check before shipping copy

1. Point every line at its source line in the `.mdx`. Un-pointable line → delete it.
2. Count the jokes. At least one per third of the piece survived? If zero, it's been
   sanitized — go back and restore them.
3. Read it out loud. Anything you'd never say to a friend on WhatsApp gets cut.
4. Is there an ask at the end that wasn't in the source? Delete it.
