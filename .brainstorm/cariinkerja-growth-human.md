# Cariinkerja — Human Growth Playbook (2026-07-15)

> **Split:** Jordan grows the **product/code** in Cariinkerja's own repo. This doc is the
> other half — **what Jordan does as a human**: distribution, retention, positioning.
> Constraints stand: **~3-5 hrs/wk**, help-first, no blasts, no dark patterns, and the
> product ethos — *don't sell to people who can't afford it.*

## Two things that shape everything

1. **Your audience is pre-qualified on LinkedIn.** Cariinkerja *requires* a LinkedIn
   profile, so every real user is LinkedIn-active. Founder-led Bahasa content on LinkedIn
   reaches exactly the right people for free — the channel *is* the filter.
2. **You already have payers — so talk to them before chasing more.** Acquisition into a
   funnel you don't understand is a leaky bucket. 5 conversations tell you why people pay
   and why free users stall. Fix that first; then traffic converts.

---

## ▶ START HERE — this week's needle-mover: talk to 5 users (~2 hrs)

Highest leverage thing you can do, full stop. Reach out (WA / short voice call) to:

- **3 paying users** — why did you pay?
- **2 who signed up free and never subscribed** — what stopped you?

Ask, in their words (don't lead):

- "What made you actually pay?" → your **real hook**.
- "What almost stopped you / made you hesitate?" → your **real objection**.
- "How would you describe it to a friend job-hunting?" → your **positioning copy**.
- "Was there a moment it nearly wasn't worth it?" → your **churn risk**.

**Output:** the actual hook + objection in customer language. Feeds your LinkedIn copy,
the `/work` proof section, pricing decisions, and tells the code repo what to build next.
Everything below gets sharper once you've done this.

## The growth loop (ongoing, priority order)

### 1. Founder LinkedIn content — Bahasa, ~2×/week
Your cheapest, compounding, pre-qualified channel. Not ads — *value + build-in-public*:
- One **real (anonymized) 0–100 verdict**: "Job ini di-score 72. Skill match, gap-nya X.
  Kenapa 72 sering lebih worth dikejar daripada 85." Shows the product thinking without a
  pitch.
- A **skill-gap teardown** for a common role (fresh-grad data analyst, backend, etc.).
- **"How to read a JD"** / how to stop applying blind — the pain Cariinkerja solves.
- Soft CTA at most 1 in 3 posts. Teach first; the tool sells itself when the thinking is
  visibly good.

### 2. Seed value in ID job-seeker communities
Telegram / Facebook / WhatsApp **loker** groups + relevant subreddits (r/indonesia,
finance/career threads). **Help first** — answer a real "gimana cara..." question, drop
the link only when it genuinely fits. One real answer > ten pasted promos. No blasts.

### 3. Referral + testimonial asks to current payers
Highest-trust, near-zero cost. When you talk to the paying users above, close each chat
with: *"Boleh minta 2 kalimat buat aku pajang? Dan — kenal orang lagi cari kerja?"* Two
outputs from one conversation: proof + a warm referral.

### 4. One partnership experiment — Sidik's academy
His students are job seekers → offer Cariinkerja (free tier + Open-to-Work discount) as a
**career-services perk** for his cohort. Warm, mutual, no cold outreach. This is the
right *shape* of deal (simple, aligned) — unlike the Ivan/Uwais builds you dropped.

## Lean into the ethos — it's marketing, not just policy

"We discount for people marked Open-to-Work" and "don't subscribe if it'd hurt your
money" are genuinely rare and share-worthy in a market full of predatory job scams. This
is a **trust differentiator** — say it out loud on LinkedIn and to any ID tech media.
Being the honest one *is* the positioning.

## What NOT to do

- No follower-buying, no engagement pods, no cold DM blasts.
- Don't sell to people who can't afford it (product ethos — hold the line).
- Don't disguise feature-building as growth. New features belong in the code repo, and
  only *after* the user talks say a missing feature is the real blocker.
