After years of writing in scattered places — Medium, LinkedIn, threads of Threads — I'm pulling everything back home.

This site is the workbench: half portfolio, half notebook. Long-form notes on things I'm building or breaking, the occasional rant about Indonesian tech hiring, and the quieter posts about how a mobile lead actually spends his Tuesdays.

A few principles for what gets published here:

- **Show the work.** If I can include code, screenshots, or a postmortem, I will.
- **Respect your time.** Short posts beat long posts. If it can be a tweet, it stays a tweet.
- **Bahasa or English, depending on the audience.** Indonesia-specific posts run in both; everything else stays in English.

Subscribe via [RSS](/rss.xml) if you want each post in your reader. The list is short for now — I'd rather have one good post per fortnight than weekly noise.
